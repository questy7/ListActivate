What Makes a Business a Good DBR Candidate

These three factors determine whether database reactivation will generate meaningful returns for your business.

01

A Large Dormant Database

The more contacts in your database, the more revenue potential on the table. We work with databases starting at 1,000 contacts — with the strongest results at 5,000+.

02

High Lead or Customer Value

Reactivation economics work best when a converted contact represents significant lifetime revenue — whether that's a signed legal case, a high-ticket coaching client, or a closed real estate deal.

03

Verified Opt-In Contact Records

We only work with contacts who originally consented to receive communications. No cold lists. If your database has clean opt-in records, you're likely a strong candidate.
