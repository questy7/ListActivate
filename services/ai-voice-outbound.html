What It Is

AI Agents That Sound Like Humans. Perform Like Machines.

AI voice outbound uses conversational AI calling agents to dial dormant contacts from your database, engage them in natural-sounding conversations, qualify their intent, and either route them to your team or book an appointment directly into your calendar.

These aren't robocalls. Modern AI voice agents carry on contextual, dynamic conversations — they can handle objections, answer common questions about your offer, adjust based on what the contact says, and escalate to a human when a live conversation is warranted.

AI voice outbound is the third layer in our reactivation stack. Contacts who didn't respond to email sequences or SMS messages often respond to a phone call. At scale, that means hundreds or thousands of qualified conversations your sales team never had to initiate.

Natural-sounding voice with low latency
Dynamic conversation — responds to what contacts say
Qualification scripts built around your offer
Live calendar booking and appointment setting
Human escalation for complex or high-intent calls

Why Voice Is the Third Touch That Converts

Email and SMS move contacts toward action. A phone call closes the gap for contacts who need one more push.

Reaches the Unconverted Segment

A meaningful portion of your dormant database will never respond to written outreach — but they'll answer a phone call. AI voice captures revenue that email and SMS leave behind.

Qualifies Intent in Real Time

The AI asks the questions your sales team would ask — gauging readiness, identifying objections, and determining which contacts are worth a live conversation right now versus a follow-up later.

Books Appointments Automatically

Ready-to-buy contacts don't need to be transferred to a human. The AI agent books the appointment directly — calendar invite sent, slot confirmed, while the contact is still on the line.

Scales Without Headcount

A human caller can make 60–80 dials per day. AI voice agents can dial thousands. A database of 10,000 dormant contacts can be worked in days, not months — with no additional sales staff required.

Every Call Recorded & Transcribed

Full recordings and transcripts for every conversation. Your team gets complete visibility into what was said, what objections came up, and which contacts are ready to close.

Runs 24/7 Without Fatigue

AI agents don't get tired, demoralized, or distracted. They deliver consistent, on-script conversations with every contact — at 2pm or 2am, weekdays or weekends.

How AI Voice Outbound Works

From script development to live campaigns — deployed as a standalone service or layered into a full reactivation system.

01

Script Development

We write the conversation script — opening, qualification questions, objection handling, booking flow, and opt-out paths. Your brand voice, your offer, your qualification criteria.

02

Agent Configuration

The AI agent is trained on your script, calendar integration is connected, escalation triggers are set, and the voice and persona are configured to fit your brand.

03

Database Dialing

Contacts are loaded, compliance checks completed, and the campaign launches. The AI dials, engages, qualifies, books, and logs every interaction automatically.

04

Hot Lead Routing

High-intent contacts are escalated to your sales team immediately. Booked appointments sync to your calendar. Transcripts and recordings delivered for every completed call.
