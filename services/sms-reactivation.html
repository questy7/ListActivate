What It Is

The Attention Channel Email Can't Touch.

SMS re-engagement campaigns are short-form, high-impact text message campaigns sent to contacts who consented to receive SMS communications. Unlike email — where inbox competition is fierce and open rates hover around 20% — SMS messages are read by almost everyone, almost immediately.

In a reactivation context, SMS serves as a powerful second touch. Contacts who didn't respond to email sequences often react to a direct, personal-feeling text. The format demands brevity: every message is focused, direct, and carries a single clear call to action.

We use SMS as a layer within a broader multi-channel reactivation system — combining it with email reactivation and AI voice outbound to maximize overall contact rates and conversions.

Hey [First Name], it's been a while. Your free consultation offer is still open — reply YES to grab a 15-min slot this week.

Compliance Is Non-Negotiable.

TCPA violations carry penalties of $500–$1,500 per message. We operate with compliance at the center of everything we do — not as an afterthought.

Express Written Consent Required

The Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) requires express written consent before sending marketing text messages. We verify that every contact on your SMS list provided this consent — before a single message goes out.

Opt-Out Honored Immediately

Every SMS includes a clear STOP opt-out mechanism. Opt-outs are processed in real time and respected permanently. No contact who has opted out ever receives another message from our platform.

Sending Window Compliance

TCPA restricts marketing SMS messages to between 8am and 9pm in the recipient's local time zone. Our platform enforces this automatically, adjusting for time zones across all contacts.

No Cold or Purchased Lists

We do not send SMS campaigns to purchased lists, cold contacts, or anyone who has not specifically opted in to receive text messages. If your list's consent status is unclear, we'll help you verify before any outreach begins.

How SMS Reactivation Works

From consent verification to live campaigns — four focused steps.
