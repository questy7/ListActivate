The Multi-Channel Advantage

Single-channel reactivation leaves revenue on the table. Different contacts respond to different channels — some to email, some to SMS, some only pick up the phone. Our layered approach ensures you reach the maximum number of contacts and drive the highest possible reactivation rate.

Layer 1

Email — Nurture & Context

Multi-step sequences establish context, deliver value, and move contacts toward action. The foundation of every reactivation campaign.

Layer 2

SMS — Attention & Urgency

For contacts who didn't open emails. SMS cuts through with direct, high-visibility messages that demand attention at a 98% open rate.

Layer 3

AI Voice — Qualification & Conversion

For contacts who need a conversation to act. AI agents qualify, answer objections, and hand off warm leads to your team or book calls directly.
