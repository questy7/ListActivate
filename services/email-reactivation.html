What It Is

Not Blasts. Structured Campaigns with Logic.

Email reactivation is the process of systematically reaching back out to contacts who have gone cold — subscribers who opted in but stopped engaging, customers who bought once and disappeared, leads who inquired but never converted.

Most businesses attempt this with a one-off blast and wonder why it doesn't work. We build behaviorally triggered multi-step sequences that adapt based on how contacts respond — or don't respond. Each email has a purpose within a larger arc designed to reestablish trust, create relevance, and drive action.

The campaigns target contacts who have been dormant for 6 months or more and are built around segmentation — different segments receive different messaging based on how and when they originally opted in.

Behaviorally triggered sequences

Emails fire based on opens, clicks, time delays, and non-engagement signals.

AI-personalized copy

Dynamic variables and personalization signals make every email feel 1:1.

Deliverability-first approach

Warm-up, authentication, list hygiene, and sending cadence managed for inbox placement.

Segment-specific messaging

Leads, past buyers, and webinar registrants each receive messaging matched to their context.

How It Works

From database audit to live campaigns in under two weeks. Here's the four-step process.

01

Segment Your Database

We analyze your list by recency, source, behavior, and prior engagement. Contacts are grouped into segments that will receive distinct messaging strategies.

02

Build Tailored Sequences

Our copywriters craft multi-email win-back sequences for each segment. Subject lines, preview text, body copy, and CTAs are all written and structured for maximum response.

03

Deploy with Personalization

Campaigns go live with AI-driven personalization at scale. Behavioral triggers route contacts through the right path based on their actions — or inactions.

04

Optimize on Response Data

Open rates, click rates, replies, and conversions are tracked and fed back into the campaign. A/B tests identify winning subject lines and angles for ongoing optimization.

Who This Is For

1,000+ contacts on a cold list

Smaller lists can work, but email reactivation produces its strongest ROI when there are meaningful numbers to work with.

Contacts dormant 6+ months

Verified opt-in contacts only
