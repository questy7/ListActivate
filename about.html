Our Story

Who Got Tired of
Wasted Lists.

We spent years watching businesses pour money into acquiring new leads while ignoring the gold mine sitting in their existing databases. A contact who once opted in, bought, or enquired is exponentially easier to reactivate than a cold stranger — yet most businesses treat them the same.

ListActivate was founded to close that gap. We specialize exclusively in database reactivation and the content that sustains engagement long after the initial campaign fires. Every strategy, every sequence, every word of copy is built around one goal: recovering revenue you've already earned the right to.

Today we combine direct-response copywriting, multi-channel sequencing, and artificial intelligence to deliver campaigns that consistently outperform the industry benchmarks — for businesses of every size.
