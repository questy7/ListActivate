We Built ListActivate to
Solve a Specific Problem.

Most businesses are sitting on thousands of lapsed customers and cold contacts — people who raised their hand once and then went quiet. That dormant contact database isn't dead. It just needs the right win-back campaign. And the revenue waiting inside it is already yours.

Our Story

Built by Marketers
Who Got Tired of
Wasted Lists.

We spent years watching businesses pour money into acquiring new leads while ignoring the gold mine sitting in their existing contact database. Lapsed customers who once bought from you are exponentially easier and cheaper to reactivate than cold prospects — yet most businesses let their dormant lists go to waste.

ListActivate was founded to close that gap. We specialize in database reactivation — win-back campaigns that combine email reactivation sequences, SMS re-engagement, AI voice outbound, ringless voicemail, and a unified messaging CRM. Every strategy is built around one goal: recovering the revenue that's already yours.

Today we serve businesses of every size, delivering multi-channel reactivation campaigns that consistently outperform industry benchmarks — and the content that keeps reactivated contacts engaged for the long run.

The Principles We Work By

Results Over Vanity Metrics

Open rates and click rates are diagnostic tools, not the goal. We measure success in revenue recovered and pipeline generated. If a campaign looks good but doesn't convert, we fix it.

Precision Personalization

Mass-blast campaigns are dead. Every message we craft is designed to feel like it was written for one specific person — because with AI-assisted segmentation, it effectively was.

Speed to Market

Revenue delayed is revenue at risk. We audit, strategize, write, and build quickly — getting campaigns live within 24 hours of sign-off so you stop losing money to inaction.

Honest Communication
