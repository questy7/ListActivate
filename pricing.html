Compliance First

We Only Reach Out
Where You Have
Permission.

This isn't a disclaimer buried in fine print. It's how we operate. Compliant outreach protects your brand, your deliverability, and your business — and it's non-negotiable for every campaign we run.

Discuss Your Compliance Setup

Documented Opt-In Required

Before any campaign launches, clients must provide verifiable proof of opt-in consent for every contact on each channel being used. No exceptions.

Email — CAN-SPAM & CASL Compliant

Email contacts must have explicitly opted in to receive marketing communications. We do not send to purchased lists or contacts without clear permission records.

SMS — TCPA Express Written Consent

SMS contacts must have provided express written consent to receive text marketing. We verify this before any SMS campaign is activated.

No Cold Lists. Ever.

We do not work with scraped data, purchased contact lists, or any list where documented consent cannot be confirmed. If you don't have consent records, we can help you build a compliant acquisition strategy first.

How does the revenue share work?

On Performance DBR campaigns, we earn a share of the revenue directly attributable to the reactivation campaign. The exact split is agreed in writing before we start anything — and discussed on your 15-minute call. If we recover nothing, you pay nothing.

How do you decide who qualifies for Performance DBR?

We hand-pick partners based on database quality, industry fit, and realistic revenue potential. During the 15-minute call we assess your situation honestly. If we don't think the campaign will perform well for both sides, we'll tell you that directly — and often point you toward a content engagement instead.

What if I don't have clear opt-in records for my list?

We won't campaign to contacts without verified consent — it protects you as much as it does us. If your records are incomplete, we'll discuss compliant options for rebuilding your permission base before any outreach begins.

Can I combine the DBR campaign with content generation?

Yes. Many partners run a Performance DBR campaign to recover immediate revenue, then move into ongoing Email & SMS Content to maintain engagement with reactivated contacts. We can structure both together.

Are monthly content fees fixed or flexible?

Monthly rates are tailored to your volume, channels, and content requirements. We don't publish fixed prices because a 4-post-per-month social package looks very different from a full blog + email + video script retainer. Your call is where we figure out what makes sense.
