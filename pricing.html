What exactly does "performance-based" mean?

You pay a pre-agreed fee only when we deliver a defined result — typically revenue recovered, appointments booked, or reactivated leads. The exact structure depends on your business model and is set before we start anything. Nothing happens without your sign-off.

Is there really zero upfront cost?

Yes. No setup fee, no deposit, no monthly retainer to get started. We invest our own time and resources to build and launch your campaign. Our income depends on your results, which is exactly how we want it.

What if my database is old or messy?

That's what we do. We've reactivated lists that have been cold for years. During the free audit we'll assess the quality of your data and give you an honest picture of what's realistically recoverable before any agreement is made.

How long before I see results?

Campaigns go live within 24 hours of sign-off. Most clients see re-engagement activity within the first 48–72 hours. Revenue recovery timelines vary based on your sales cycle, but the pipeline starts moving fast.

What's the 15-minute call about?

It's a quick, no-obligation conversation to understand your database, your goals, and your business model. We use that to tell you honestly whether we're a fit and what kind of results you can expect — before anything is agreed.
