Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 9, 2026  ·  Last Updated: June 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information about you is collected, used, and disclosed by ListActivate (“ListActivate,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) when you use https://listactivate.com and related services, including website features, onboarding, and database reactivation services (collectively, the “Services”).

This business currently operates as ListActivate.

By using the Services, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

1. Scope

This policy applies to personal information collected:

  • directly from you;
  • automatically through use of the Services;
  • from third-party integrations and providers;
  • in connection with paid engagements and support.

2. Information You Provide

We may collect information you provide directly, such as:

  • name, email, phone, business name, address, and account credentials;
  • billing and contact information;
  • information in forms, onboarding, support requests, and communications;
  • campaign-related content you submit or authorize.

Payment card data is typically processed by third-party processors (for example Stripe) under their own privacy terms: https://stripe.com/privacy.

3. SMS and Text Message Communications

If you provide your mobile phone number and consent to receive text messages from ListActivate, we may send you SMS communications including appointment reminders, follow-ups, and service-related messages.

  • Consent: By providing your phone number and opting in, you consent to receive text messages from ListActivate. Consent is not a condition of purchase.
  • Message Frequency: Message frequency varies based on your interactions with us.
  • Rates: Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your carrier and plan.
  • Opt-Out: You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. You will receive a one-time confirmation and no further messages will be sent.
  • Help: Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at info@listactivate.com.
  • No Sharing: We do not sell, share, rent, or otherwise disclose your phone number or SMS opt-in data to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Information Collected Automatically

We may collect:

  • log and device data (IP address, browser, pages viewed, timestamps, referring URLs);
  • usage analytics and interaction events;
  • cookie and similar technology data.

5. Information from Other Sources

We may receive information from partners, integrations, analytics providers, advertising providers, and publicly available sources, and combine it with information we already hold.

6. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • provide, maintain, secure, and improve Services;
  • create and manage accounts and authenticate users;
  • process transactions, invoices, and support;
  • communicate about service updates, security, and operations;
  • send marketing where permitted by law;
  • monitor, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and legal violations;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce agreements.

7. How We Disclose Information

We may disclose personal information:

  • to service providers and contractors processing data on our behalf;
  • to integration partners you authorize;
  • to advisors (legal, accounting, compliance);
  • when required by law, legal process, or to protect rights and safety;

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We may use analytics and advertising tools to understand usage and improve Services. Providers may include Google, Meta/Facebook, and similar platforms. You can manage certain ad preferences via browser and device settings and provider opt-out tools.
