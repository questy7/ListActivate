Industries We Serve

Built for Businesses
With Something to Recover

Database reactivation works best where contact lists are large, lead value is high, and there's a real cost to leaving dormant contacts untouched.

Mass Tort & Class Action Law Firms

Claimant databases are among the highest-value lists in existence. Firms collect thousands of opt-in contacts through advertising campaigns — many of whom never convert. We reactivate them at scale with compliant, structured outreach sequences.

Business Coaching & Consulting

Coaches and consultants build large lists through webinars, lead magnets, and free challenges — then watch them go cold. These contacts already know you. A well-timed reactivation sequence converts a fraction of them into high-ticket clients.

Real Estate & Mortgage

Past buyer inquiries, expired leads, and rate-change triggers make real estate one of the highest-ROI verticals for reactivation. Mortgage professionals use us to re-engage pre-qualified contacts when market conditions shift in their favor.

Financial Services & Insurance

Policy renewals, past prospects, and cross-sell opportunities sit dormant in most agency CRMs. We design compliant outreach that re-engages former clients and warm leads with relevant, timely offers — without burning your relationship with them.

Med Spa & Aesthetic Clinics

Patient lifetime value is high and seasonal. Clinics with established contact databases can recover significant revenue by reactivating past patients around treatment cycles, new service launches, or seasonal promotions — with permission-based outreach.

Home Services (HVAC, Solar, Roofing)

Seasonal demand and high average ticket values make home services ideal for reactivation campaigns. Past estimate requests, former customers due for maintenance, or leads that went cold during a slow season represent real, recoverable revenue.
